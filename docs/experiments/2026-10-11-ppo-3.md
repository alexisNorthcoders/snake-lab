# ppo-3: draw and loss penalties (fewer draws only a little; every draw is a head-on)

ppo-2 again, with a penalty for drawing and a bigger one for losing, to make Munch Max's replacement draw
less. It wins a little more and loses a little less, but **draws against the rookie barely fell**, and a
sample shows why: every draw was a head-on collision.

## The run

- **Dates:** 2026-10-10 22:34 to 2026-10-11 09:29 (BST), with the machine suspended from 22:55 to 08:52;
  about 58 minutes of training. A suspended run just carries on when the machine wakes.
- **Machine and engine:** as for [ppo-2](2026-10-10-ppo-1-and-2.md), at `engine-v4.3.2` (so the gauntlet
  also plays Greedy Gus and Munch Max). Code `27e09ef`.

  `nice -n 19 .venv/bin/python -m snake_lab.run --personality glutton --run ../runs/ppo-3 --seed 1 --rookie-updates 2000 --league-updates 2000`
- **Settings:** ppo-2's, with `glutton_draw_penalty` 100 and `glutton_loss_penalty` 150 (a win still +200),
  off in the alone stage. 808 checkpoints, 8.3M steps. The CPU stayed at 52–66°C.

## Why it still draws

Of checkpoint 707's matches against the rookie at delay 2 on seeds 1 to 40 (both modes, 80 matches), the 41
draws were **all head-on collisions, both snakes dying on the same tick**. None was a time-up with both alive.
At a reaction delay of 2 ticks each snake acts on a board two ticks old, so two snakes heading for the
same cell often can't see it coming in time, and the rules make a head-on a draw.

## What it tells us

1. **The penalties helped a little.** The best checkpoints win 35–37% against the rookie (ppo-2: 33.5%),
   lose as little as 12.8%, and beat Munch Max about 45% to 27%. Unlike ppo-2, the league kept improving
   it after checkpoint 500.
2. **They didn't fix the draws.** Draws against the rookie are 45–52%, as before.
3. **The draws come from the rules, not the reward.** Every sampled draw was a head-on. Training can only
   shave them; a rule deciding a head-on (say, the longer snake survives) would remove most of them.
