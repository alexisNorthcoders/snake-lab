# ppo-1 and ppo-2: the first PPO runs (no checkpoint clears the bar, but the best yet)

The first PPO runs for snake-lab#26: a short one to try the pipeline, then a long one with a win bonus.
**None passed** the 60% bar. ppo-2's best checkpoint won 33.5% against the rookie at delay 2 and lost
only 17.8%: it beats the rookie more often than it loses to it, which no evolved snake did.

## The runs

- **Date:** 2026-10-10 (BST).
- **Machine:** the explorer machine, a Ryzen 5 2600 (12 threads), Python 3.12.3, PyTorch 2.14.1 (the CPU
  build: the RX 580 has no PyTorch support, and the network is about 5% of the work anyway), Node v22.18.0.
- **Engine:** `snake-colyseus` at `engine-v4.3.1`, so the gauntlet also plays Greedy Gus.
- **Settings:** the defaults (encoder v2, 16 matches a batch, 128 ticks a rollout, hidden layers 64 and
  64, 5 updates a checkpoint, one torch thread), Glutton reward, seed 1.

| Run | Code | Stages (updates) | Win bonus | Steps | Time | CPU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ppo-1 | `e7166f4` | 40 alone, 160 rookie, 200 league | none | 819,200 | about 5 minutes | up to 60°C |
| ppo-2 | `82205ce` | 40 alone, 2,000 rookie, 2,000 league | 200 | 8,273,920 | about 60 minutes | up to 62°C |

Both were run from `python/` as
`nice -n 19 .venv/bin/python -m snake_lab.run --personality glutton --run ../runs/ppo-N --seed 1 ...`
with the stage lengths above (`--rookie-updates`, `--league-updates`). About 2,300 steps a second.

## What it tells us

1. **PPO with encoder v2 is the strongest snake so far.** ppo-2's checkpoint 507 wins about twice as often
   as it loses against the rookie, and beats Greedy Gus two times in three.
2. **The win bonus and the longer run both mattered.** ppo-1 (no bonus, a tenth of the steps) mostly drew;
   ppo-2 wins twice as often, with fewer draws.
3. **Draws are what stand between it and 60%.** About half of its matches against the rookie are draws.
   The rookie draws 84% of its matches against itself, so the bar asks a snake to break through a very
   defensive opponent on most seeds. Counting a draw as half a win, checkpoint 507 scores 57.9%.
4. **The league added little.** The return flattened from about checkpoint 500, and the last league
   checkpoints are no better than 507. More of the same training isn't likely to reach 60%.
