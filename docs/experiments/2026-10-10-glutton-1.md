# glutton-1: the first real Glutton run (no generation clears the bar)

The first full neuroevolution run for snake-lab#11. 500 generations at the default settings, then the
gauntlet on four of them. **None passed**: the best won 27.8% against the rookie at delay 2, against a
bar of 60%.

## The run

- **Dates:** 2026-10-09 23:40 to 2026-10-10 01:04 (BST).
- **Machine:** the explorer machine, a Ryzen 5 2600 (12 threads), Node v22.18.0.
- **Engine:** `snake-colyseus` at `engine-v4.2.0`.
- **Code:** generations 0–126 on `1a4a1d1`, 127–499 on `c496f51` (the worker fix, #36). The number of
  workers never changes a result, so the run is one run.
- **Command:**
  `nice -n 19 npx tsx src/cli/train.ts --personality glutton --run runs/glutton-1 --seed 1 --generations 500 --workers 1`,
  resumed with `--resume runs/glutton-1` at `--workers 8`, then at `--workers 1` again.
- **Settings:** the defaults of that time except `generations`: 10 alone, 20 against the rookie, then
  the league (a fifth of its matches four-player); a population of 50, 2 elites, tournaments of 3, a
  mutation rate of 0.1 and size of 0.2; one hidden layer of 16 (`tanh`); 10 matches a snake a
  generation at 8 fps; Glutton fitness = food score + 0.1 a tick survived, **with no bonus for winning**.
- **Size:** 500 × 50 × 10 = 250,000 matches.

a long run here goes on 1 worker, with a guard that stops it cleanly at 85°C.

## Fitness

The mean is the whole population's; the best is each generation's fittest, from only 10 matches, so
it's noisy. Averages over blocks of 25 generations:

| Generations | Mean | Best |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30–54 | 200 | 329 |
| 80–104 | 322 | 550 |
| 105–129 | 390 | 637 |
| 155–179 | 411 | 646 |
| 230–254 | 481 | 701 |
| 330–354 | 502 | 724 |
| 355–379 | 468 | 699 |

Quick progress to about generation 130, a slow climb to about 300, then flat. Within each stage the
mean dips when the job gets harder (generation 10, the rookie; generation 30, the league) and then
recovers. In the league, half of the opponents come from the population itself, so a flat mean can
hide a stronger population.

## The gauntlet

`npm run gauntlet -- runs/glutton-1/generations/gen-NNNN.json` at the defaults (100 seeds, each in both
seats, at 8 fps): 4,000 matches against the rookie and the dummy, about 2.5 minutes each on one core.
The verdict is wins against the rookie at delay 2, both modes:

| Generation | Win | Loss | Draw | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 130 | 10.5% | 48.5% | 41.0% | fail |
| 250 | 21.0% | 45.0% | 34.0% | fail |
| 385 | **27.8%** | 47.3% | 25.0% | fail |
| 499 | 11.3% | 40.0% | 48.8% | fail |

The whole tables are kept with the run, in `runs/glutton-1/gauntlet/`. Two other things in them:

- At delay 0 the rookie wins 51–70% of the matches against every generation: with no reaction delay
  its "never step beside a head" rule is very hard to beat.
- Generation 499 draws about half of its matches, against the dummy too: the last generation's best is
  whichever snake was luckiest in its 10 matches, not the strongest.

## What it tells us

1. **The snakes improved for real**, 10.5% to 27.8% between generations 130 and 385, more than the
   flat fitness curve suggested.
2. **The last generation isn't the best**, so `best.json` is the wrong snake to promote: run the
   gauntlet over several saved generations and promote the best of them.
3. **The fitness doesn't ask for wins.** A Glutton was scored on food and survival only, and the
   gauntlet asks it to beat the rookie. The best snake still loses to the rookie about half the time.
4. The senses (encoder v1: 3 cells of lookahead, no memory) still let it walk into traps the rookie's
   rules avoid. More generations at these settings aren't likely to reach 60%.

## What changed after it

- `gluttonWinBonus` (default 200): a Glutton now scores a bonus for every match it wins. A run saved
  before it reads it as 0, so glutton-1 still resumes exactly as it was trained.
  [glutton-2](2026-10-10-glutton-2.md) tried it, and it didn't raise the best win rate.

Still open: whether 60% is a fair bar for encoder v1 (the rookie draws 84% of its matches against
itself at delay 2), and PPO with encoder v2 (snake-lab#26).
