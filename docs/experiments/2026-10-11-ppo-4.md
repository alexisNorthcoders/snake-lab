## What it tells us

1. **The rule change mattered more than any reward change.** Draws went from 45–52% to 6–9%, and the best
   win rate against the rookie from 36.5% to 59.5%.
2. **Training under the new rule added to it.** ppo-3's 607, re-run under v3, won 53.0%; ppo-4's best won
   59.5% and beats Munch Max about 60% to 36%.
3. **It's at the bar, not over it.** 607 and 707 are about equal: 707 is best at delay 2, 607 across all
   delays and against the other bots.
