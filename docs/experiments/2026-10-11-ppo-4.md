# ppo-4: the first run under rules v3 (the longer snake wins a head-on)

[ppo-3](2026-10-11-ppo-3.md) again, under rules v3 (alexisNorthcoders/snake-colyseus#94, `engine-v5.0.0`),
where the longer snake survives a head-on and only equal lengths both die. Draws against the rookie fell
from about half to about 6%, and **checkpoint 707 won 59.5%, just short of the 60% bar**.

## The rule change, on the old snakes

Before training, the gauntlet re-ran three existing snakes under rules v3 (`runs/rules-v3/`). Against the
rookie at delay 2, both modes (win, loss, draw):

| Snake | Rules v2 | Rules v3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Munch Max (ppo-2's 507) | 33.5% / 17.8% / 48.8% | 46.5% / 48.5% / 5.0% |
| ppo-3's 607 | 36.5% / 18.8% / 44.8% | 53.0% / 41.8% / 5.3% |
| Greedy Gus | about 23% wins | 23.3% / 76.0% / 0.8% |

Almost every draw became a win for one side or the other.

## What it tells us

1. **The rule change mattered more than any reward change.** Draws went from 45–52% to 6–9%, and the best
   win rate against the rookie from 36.5% to 59.5%.
2. **Training under the new rule added to it.** ppo-3's 607, re-run under v3, won 53.0%; ppo-4's best won
   59.5% and beats Munch Max about 60% to 36%.
3. **It's at the bar, not over it.** 607 and 707 are about equal: 707 is best at delay 2, 607 across all
   delays and against the other bots.
