# glutton-2: the win bonus (no generation clears the bar)

glutton-1 again, with one change: `gluttonWinBonus` 200, so a Glutton scores a bonus for every match it
wins ([glutton-1](2026-10-10-glutton-1.md) had none). **None passed**: the best won 23.5% against the
rookie at delay 2, against a bar of 60%, and glutton-1's best was 27.8%.

## The run

- **Dates:** 2026-10-10 10:44 to 12:38 (BST).
- **Machine:** the explorer machine, a Ryzen 5 2600 (12 threads), Node v22.18.0, at 49–52°C throughout.
- **Engine:** `snake-colyseus` at `engine-v4.2.0`.
- **Code:** `c6b91ba`.
- **Command:**
  `nice -n 19 npx tsx src/cli/train.ts --personality glutton --run runs/glutton-2 --seed 1 --generations 500 --workers 1`
- **Settings:** glutton-1's, with `gluttonWinBonus` 200.
- **Time:** 6,851 seconds for 250,000 matches: 36.5 matches a second, 263 generations an hour.

## What it tells us

1. **The win bonus didn't raise the ceiling.** The best of four generations won 23.5%, against
   glutton-1's 27.8%. Both runs top out between 20% and 30%.
2. **It made the generations more even.** Its worst checked generation won 15.8% (glutton-1's, 10.5%),
   and the last one is no longer the weakest: generation 499 draws a third of its matches, not half.
3. The difference between a run's checked generations (16–24% here, 11–28% in glutton-1) is about as big
   as the difference between the runs, so one run's ranking of its generations is mostly noise at this
   level.
4. That leaves the senses (encoder v1) and the bar as the limits, not the fitness. A snake meant to be
   the easiest opponent doesn't need to beat the rookie 60% of the time, but `promote` refuses any snake
   that fails the gauntlet.
